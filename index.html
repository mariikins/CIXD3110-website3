04 / Water, recently returned

Thirteen
basins.
One long exhale.

First flowing in 1932, the cascade widens one step at a time to exaggerate its own length. After seven quiet years, the repaired fountain and reflecting pool reopened on May 14, 2026.

05 / The unofficial institution

Sunday
belongs
to rhythm.

On Sunday afternoons, the upper park becomes a loose choreography: drumming, dancing, dogs, yoga, soccer, picnics, old friends and accidental new ones.

The gathering is living culture, not a performance scheduled for you. Bring curiosity, share the lawn, and let people have their moment.
